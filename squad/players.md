## What the numbers say

- **Two world-class players carry the team.** Guillaume and Pequignot are 20 in almost every attribute that matters and are 5★ in every central role. Everyone else is 2 to 3★.
- **No centre-back can pass.** Passing is 5 (Mrabet), 7 (Jehl) and 8 (Buswita). Ayyouch (13) is the only exception, and he is a 2★ player.
- **Full-backs are the weakest unit.** Every natural full-back or wing-back is 2★. That is why a back three suits this squad.
- **No natural goalscoring striker.** Perruchon (Fin 11) is the best fit. Guillaume (Fin 20, OtB 20) scores the goals from AMC.
- **Leaving in the summer:** Payen (Valladolid), Julen (West Brom), Quesada-Thorn and Fernandez (retiring). Llor and Akognon's contracts also end in June 2040.
- **Wage bill:** €16.34M p/a against a €14.41M budget (from the home screen).
